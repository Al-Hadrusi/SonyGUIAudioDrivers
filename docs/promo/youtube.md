## Description

```text
Headphones Control is a free, open-source desktop app for Sony Bluetooth headphones. Switch noise canceling, shape your sound with a 10-band equalizer and change Listening Mode, right from your Linux or Windows PC.

▶ Download (free): https://github.com/Al-Hadrusi/SonyGUIAudioDrivers/releases
★ Source code: https://github.com/Al-Hadrusi/SonyGUIAudioDrivers

WHAT IT DOES
• Noise Canceling, Ambient Sound and Off, with ambient level, Focus on Voice and Auto Ambient Sound
• 10-band equalizer (WH-1000XM6) or Clear Bass + 5 bands, with presets and your own curves
• Listening Mode: Standard, BGM (with room size) and Cinema
• DSEE Extreme, Speak-to-Chat, Bluetooth connection quality and voice guidance
• Touch panel, multipoint and the other settings your headset offers
• Battery levels, play/pause/skip and volume
• System tray icon, low-battery notifications and automatic reconnect

BUILT DIFFERENTLY
Written in 100% safe Rust: the compiler rejects any unsafe code, and every message from the headset is checked before it is used. Free and open source under the MIT license.

WORKS WITH
Sony headphones and earbuds that use Sony's headset control protocol, such as the WH-1000X and WF-1000X series (tested on the WH-1000XM6). Runs on Linux (BlueZ) and Windows 10/11. Gentoo users: an ebuild is included.

Music: original soundtrack, made for this video.

Headphones Control is an independent, unofficial project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Sony. Sony and Sony product names are trademarks of their respective owners.

#OpenSource #Rust #Linux
```
